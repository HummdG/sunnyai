This is the document the demo tenant's assistant answers from when a visitor asks
something the catalogue cannot answer: which postcodes are covered, what the
warranty actually says, how long a survey takes, and what happens after a
complaint. It is chunked by heading, so each section below is a passage the
assistant can quote on its own.

## Service areas

We install across Leicestershire, Rutland, Northamptonshire, and the southern
half of Nottinghamshire. LE, NN, PE and NG postcodes are our normal working
area: a survey there is booked as a standard appointment and there is no travel
charge.

We will travel further for larger systems, at a rate we agree before the survey.
If a property is outside the four counties, ask us before assuming we cover it —
we would rather say no at the start than after a survey.

## What we install

Roof-mounted solar panels with or without battery storage, hybrid and
battery-ready inverters, and EV charge points on the same supply. We fit panels
to pitched and flat roofs, including in-roof systems on new builds and
replacements where the roof covering is being renewed anyway.

We do not install ground-mount arrays, wind turbines, or off-grid systems with no
grid connection. We do not work on listed buildings without the consent
paperwork already in place, and we do not remove or re-fit roof coverings as part
of a solar job.

## How a survey works

A standard survey takes sixty to ninety minutes on site. We look at the roof
structure and covering, the consumer unit, the meter position and the cable
route, and we take measurements rather than estimating from a satellite image.

You will normally have the written proposal within two working days of the
survey. The proposal states the panel count and model, the inverter, whether a
battery is included, the expected annual generation, and a price that holds for
thirty days. Site-specific things that can change the price — a roof that needs
scaffolding on a slope, a long cable run to the meter — are listed in the
proposal rather than discovered on the day.

Installation is usually four to eight weeks after you accept the proposal, and a
typical domestic system is fitted in one day. Scaffolding goes up the day before
and comes down the day after.

## Warranty

Panels carry a manufacturer's product warranty of twenty-five years, and an
output warranty that guarantees at least 84% of the original rated power at
year twenty-five. Inverters carry ten years as standard, extendable to twenty at
extra cost. Batteries carry ten years, subject to the manufacturer's throughput
limits.

Our own workmanship is guaranteed for ten years: the fixings, the roof seals, and
the wiring we installed. The warranty covers the labour to diagnose and replace
a covered component. It does not cover damage from storm or impact, work done on
the system by someone else, or a fault caused by the roof itself — a leak at a
flashing that has been there since before the panels went on is a roofing job,
not a solar one.

Battery capacity losses from normal cycling are not a fault. Every battery
degrades, and the manufacturer's stated cycle life is what the warranty is
measured against.

## Payment and finance

We take a deposit of 20% when you accept the proposal, and the balance on the day
of installation. We accept bank transfer and card. We do not offer credit
ourselves; finance is arranged through a third party, and if you want it we will
introduce you and the application is theirs to approve or decline.

We are VAT-registered. Panel and battery installations on domestic properties
are charged at the reduced rate of 5%, and the proposal shows the VAT separately
so you can see what it is.

## After installation

We register the installation for the relevant certification and tell your
network operator, which is usually done within ten working days. You get the
handover pack by email: the certificates, the datasheets, the warranty terms, and
how to read the monitoring app.

If something is wrong, ring us or use the contact form. A fault that stops the
system generating is attended within two working days. Anything else is booked
into the next available slot, which is normally within two weeks. If the system
is generating less than the proposal predicted, we will look at the monitoring
data with you before booking a visit — the answer is more often a change in what
the house is using than a fault.

## Complaints

Tell us first and we will try to put it right. If we cannot, we are a member of
the relevant certification scheme and you can take it to them. Our complaints
procedure and the scheme's contact details are in the handover pack.
